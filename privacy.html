BOBOBs backup: privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

BOBOBs backup is used by one person, its owner, to back up their own files.

  • It collects no data about anyone. It has no user accounts, analytics, cookies or ads.
  • The only Google user data it uses is the owner's own Google Drive, through the drive.file permission. With that permission it can reach only the files it created itself.
  • Files are encrypted on the owner's own computer before upload. The encryption keys stay with the owner.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to anyone, or used for advertising.
  • The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
